                 (see LICENSE-liberation-fonts.txt)

Full licence text: https://scripts.sil.org/OFL

These files are bundled deliberately rather than fetched at runtime:
librsvg resolves font families exclusively through fontconfig and
ignores @font-face inside the SVG, so on a host with no installed fonts
(Vercel serverless) an unbundled family renders as tofu boxes.

The measured advance-width factors in src/lib/kdl-typography.ts are
derived from these exact files — re-run scripts/measure-font-metrics.ts
after changing or replacing any of them.
